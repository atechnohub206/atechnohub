Good to Know

How SIP Compounding Works

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan means investing a fixed amount into a mutual fund every month, rather than all at once.

Why It Grows Faster

Each month's investment earns returns, and those returns earn further returns — compounding accelerates growth over longer periods.

Longer Is Stronger

Small increases in your investment period can meaningfully grow your final corpus — try extending your tenure to see the effect.
