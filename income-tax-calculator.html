Good to Know

How This Calculator Works

Progressive Slabs

Pakistan uses a progressive tax system — only the portion of your income within each slab is taxed at that slab's rate.

Salary Only

This calculator assumes 100% salary income under Section 149 and doesn't include rental, business or capital gains income.

Estimate, Not Filing

Actual tax withheld may differ due to tax credits (e.g. pension fund, Zakat) — always confirm with your payroll or FBR.
