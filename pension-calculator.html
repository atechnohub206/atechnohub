Good to Know

How Pension & Commutation Work

Gross Pension Formula

A common rule of thumb: 7 × years of service × last basic pay ÷ 300, capped at 75% of your last pay drawn.

Commutation

You can convert part of your monthly pension (commonly up to 35%) into a one-time lump sum, calculated as commuted monthly pension × 12 × a commutation factor.

Commutation Factor

This factor is set by an official table based on your age at retirement. It typically ranges roughly between 8 and 11 — check the official calculator for your exact value.
